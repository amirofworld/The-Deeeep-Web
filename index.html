THE ABYSS OF CYBERSPACE

Separating cyber reality from exaggerated myths: Understanding surface web, deep web, and encrypted darknet infrastructure.

1. Surface Web vs. Deep Web vs. Dark Web

The Surface Web makes up roughly 4-5% of the total internet. The Deep Web includes unindexed databases, medical portals, and bank records. The Dark Web is a tiny specialized fraction accessible only through encrypted routing protocols.

2. Tor Browser & The Onion Routing Network

The Onion Router (Tor) wraps internet traffic in multiple layers of encryption, bouncing packets through distributed volunteer nodes (guard, middle, and exit relays) to obscure IP identity and physical location.

🔒 The .onion Protocol: Websites on the dark web do not use standard DNS. Instead, they operate with cryptographically generated hashes ending with the .onion top-level domain.

3. The Real Concerns & Cybersecurity Risks

While the Dark Web facilitates whistleblower safety, political activism, and journalists under oppressive regimes, it also harbors unregulated illicit marketplaces, credential dumps, and advanced ransomware syndicates.
